the route layer of telegram culture
t.me is the native doorway of Telegram. With over 950 million monthly active users, Telegram is one of the largest messaging platforms on Earth. Every handle, bot, channel, group, mini app, gift, and movement eventually becomes a route. $t.me is the tokenized myth of that route. Not one channel. Not one dog. Not one app. The prefix before every movement. It is... everything.
t.me/users
Every Telegram account can claim a unique @username, turning it into a public address accessible at t.me/username. Before the blockchain, before wallets or mini apps, the handle was the first digital identity people owned on Telegram. In late 2022, Telegram integrated with TON to make usernames tradeable assets through the Fragment marketplace. Premium handles have sold for over a million dollars. A username is no longer just a name. It is a scarce, ownable, publicly routable address on a network approaching one billion people.
t.me/bots
Telegram launched its Bot API in 2015, turning every chat into a programmable interface. Bots can process payments, answer inline queries, moderate groups with hundreds of thousands of members, run games, and connect to external APIs. There are millions of active bots on the platform. They handle everything from crypto price alerts and wallet interactions to customer support and automated trading. With the introduction of Web App support, bots became the gateway to Telegram's mini app ecosystem, allowing developers to embed full interactive applications inside any chat.
t.me/channels
Telegram channels are one-to-many broadcast tools with no upper limit on subscribers. Some channels reach millions of followers. They function as public news feeds, market signal streams, political broadcast networks, and community bulletin boards. Channels support reactions, comments via linked discussion groups, view counts, and scheduled posts. In many countries, Telegram channels have become primary news infrastructure, often outpacing traditional media. They are used by governments, protest movements, financial analysts, and crypto communities alike. A channel is a public route. The t.me/ link is how people find it.
t.me/groups
Telegram groups support up to 200,000 members in a single chat. They include admin hierarchies, granular permissions, anti-spam tools, slow mode, forum-style topics, and pinned messages. Groups are where Telegram communities actually operate. Trading alpha gets shared, DAOs coordinate, meme coins get raided, and movements get organized. Forum topics, introduced in 2022, allow large groups to run structured conversations across multiple threads within a single group. Every public group has a t.me/ link. Every private group can generate invite links through the same route.
t.me/miniapps
Telegram Mini Apps (originally Telegram Web Apps) launched in 2022 and expanded rapidly through 2023 and 2024. They allow developers to build full web applications that run inside Telegram with native access to user identity, payments, and the TON blockchain. Mini apps like Notcoin, Hamster Kombat, and Catizen onboarded tens of millions of users into crypto through Telegram alone. Developers can launch mini apps through bots or attach them to direct links. The t.me/ route became executable. A link is no longer just a destination. It is an application, a game, a wallet, a marketplace.
t.me/fragment
Fragment is Telegram's official auction and marketplace platform, launched in October 2022 and built on the TON blockchain. It allows users to buy and sell Telegram usernames, collectible phone numbers, and anonymous numbers using Toncoin. Fragment established that Telegram identity has monetary value. Premium four- and five-letter usernames have traded for significant sums. Anonymous numbers let users create Telegram accounts without revealing a personal phone number. Fragment turned the address layer of Telegram, the @username, the route, into a provably scarce, blockchain-verified digital asset.
t.me/memes
Telegram has one of the most active sticker and meme ecosystems of any platform. Custom sticker packs, animated stickers, custom emoji, and GIF search are built into every chat. Meme culture on Telegram travels through channels, gets amplified in groups, and spreads via sticker packs that anyone can create and share with a single t.me/ link. In the crypto space, Telegram is where meme coins launch, communities form, raids get coordinated, and narratives take shape before they ever reach Twitter or the charts. The culture moves through Telegram routes first.
